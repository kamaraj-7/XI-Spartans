Who We Are

Built On The Field,
Forged As A Family

XI Spartans is a professional club cricket outfit competing in weekend leagues and open tournaments on both turf and matted wickets. What began as a group of friends chasing a Sunday game has grown into a structured club with four active squads, a coaching set-up, and a season that runs almost year-round.

We train hard, travel further for good cricket, and play every match like a final. On and off the field, the Spartans code is simple: discipline first, ego never, and the team above the individual.

Our Story

Our Journey

Seven seasons, four squads, one badge. Here's how the Spartans went from a park kickabout to a league-winning club.

2019

Club Founded

Fourteen founding members pooled kit bags and rented their first matted ground on the edge of the city.

2020

First Tournament

Entered our maiden knockout tournament and reached the semi-finals in our debut season.

2022

Championship

Lifted our first silverware, winning the city T20 championship undefeated through the knockouts.

2024

League Winners

Topped the weekend league table across turf and matted divisions, our best season on record.

2026

Future Goals

Building a dedicated academy for junior Spartans and chasing our first national-level tournament berth.
